## Abstract
Devices, systems, and methods for augmented reality in surgical environments include an optical lens, which may be wearable by the user, and an augmented reality control system for generating an augmented reality presentation in the optical lens. The augmented reality presentation can depict the surgical area of the patient while artificially omitting one or more obstructions from the user's view point of the surgical area.
