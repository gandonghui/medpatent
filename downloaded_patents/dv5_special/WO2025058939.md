- **Date**: 2025-03-20
- **CPC**: A61B1/126, A61B1/00042, A61B90/70

## Abstract
A wiper body for an apparatus adapted to clean an imaging element of a surgical instrument comprises a first arcuate wiper edge segment and a second arcuate wiper edge segment. The first arcuate wiper edge segment has a first direction of curvature. The second arcuate wiper edge segment has a second direction of curvature segment opposite the direction of curvature of the first arcuate wiper edge segment. The second arcuate wiper edge segment extends contiguously from the first arcuate wiper edge segment to jointly define a cleaning edge of the wiper body.
